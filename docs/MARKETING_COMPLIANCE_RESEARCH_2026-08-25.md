# Malaysia EA Marketing Compliance Research — 25 August 2026

## Scope and decision boundary

This research supports an approval-gated marketing plan for FizuxCoder Expert Advisors. It does not determine whether the products, broker relationships, or any audience-specific offer require a Malaysian licence or other authorization. That determination should be confirmed with qualified Malaysian counsel or the relevant regulator before paid campaign launch.

## Official findings

| Source | Finding | Campaign control derived from the finding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Securities Commission Malaysia — Advertising and Promotion page | The SC lists its Guidelines on Advertising for Capital Market Products and Related Services as revised on 27 March 2025 and effective 1 November 2025. | Before paid launch, review the final landing page, Threads scripts, creatives, broker referral wording, disclosures, and campaign destination against the current guidelines and legal advice. |
| Securities Commission Malaysia — Investor Alert List | The SC states that unauthorised entities and individuals listed may leave investors without protection under Malaysian securities laws and advises users not to deal with them. | Do not claim authorisation, licence, regulated status, or broker approval unless documentary evidence supports it. Include a visible risk disclosure and avoid language resembling a managed investment, guaranteed system, or money-handling offer. |
| Meta — Financial and Insurance Products and Services | Meta may require business/identity verification and regulatory authorization for financial products or services; it requires applicable disclosures. Meta prohibits advertising binary options and contract-for-difference trading. | Treat paid Meta advertising of a forex/CFD EA as high-risk for approval. Do **not** launch a direct paid offer for trading, CFD, or investment returns until Meta eligibility and Malaysian regulatory review are confirmed. Use organic educational content first. |
| Meta — Advertising Standards | Ads and linked landing pages are reviewed and may be re-reviewed; deceptive or misleading practices may cause rejection or business-asset restrictions. | No earnings promises, profit screenshots without context, urgency, “guaranteed”, “passive income”, “secret system”, or claims that imply personal financial status. Every creative and landing-page variant enters an approval queue before publishing. |
| Meta — Threads API and Posts documentation | Threads supports programmatic text, image, video, and carousel publishing. A text post is limited to 500 characters. Image/video URLs must be publicly available, and publication requires media-container creation followed by publishing. | Automation can prepare and schedule drafts, but a compliant implementation should require owner approval before publish. Store content, asset URLs, risk disclosure, approval timestamp, post ID, and measured results in an audit log. |
| DataReportal — Digital 2026: Malaysia | The report states that Malaysia had 35.4 million internet users and 30.7 million social-media user identities in late 2025; it cautions that platform audience figures are not the same as unique active users. | Begin with mobile-first organic educational content and measure landing-page quality, not vanity reach alone. Treat platform audience estimates as planning context rather than a promise of available or engaged buyers. |

## Initial content restrictions

1. Do not advertise a promise of returns, win rate, profit factor, “guaranteed” results, capital protection, or risk-free automation.
2. Do not solicit deposits, broker passwords, bank details, or card information in social content, direct messages, or lead forms.
3. Do not describe FizuxCoder as a licensed broker, fund manager, investment adviser, or regulated service unless independently verified.
4. Keep the Pepperstone referral distinct from EA purchase; label it clearly as an optional broker referral and never as a condition of access.
5. Direct all product requests to the existing risk-first landing page and portal, not to a direct-message conversion path.
