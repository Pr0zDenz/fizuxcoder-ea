# FizuxCoder Threads and Creative Campaign Pack

> **Use rule.** Each item is a draft for owner approval, not a published post or ad. Do not remove the risk disclosure, add performance claims, or use broker, licence, or regulatory wording that has not been independently verified.

## Voice and guardrails

The campaign voice is concise, technical, calm, and bilingual where helpful: Malaysian English first, followed by short Bahasa Melayu lines where a plain-language clarification improves accessibility. The work should sound like an operator explaining a tool—not a promoter selling certainty.

Every post should direct the reader to the public system briefing or risk notes. Do not direct readers to deposit pages, personal messages, broker credentials, or a promise of individual trading outcomes.

## Paid-campaign draft—**not for launch**

Paid Meta advertising should remain in draft until the owner has received written regulatory and platform eligibility confirmation. Meta’s policy expressly prohibits ads promoting CFD trading, so no paid creative should present the EA as an instrument for CFD trading or a route to financial returns.[1]

If a reviewer approves a brand/education-only campaign, use this draft concept:

| Field | Draft specification |
| --- | --- |
| Objective | Educational landing-page visits, not messages, deposits, purchases, or leads requesting sensitive data. |
| Destination | `https://fizuxea-jxctlods.manus.space/` with a UTM-labelled system-briefing link and visible risk notes. |
| Audience | Adults in Malaysia only, using only targeting controls permitted by the platform and approved by the owner. Do not target financial hardship, losses, income status, or vulnerable attributes. |
| Headline | “Understand the setup before you automate.” |
| Primary text | “Explore a risk-first MT5 automation workflow with documented setup, protected delivery, and account-bound access. Review the system briefing and risk notes before deciding whether it fits your operating process.” |
| Description | “Software workflow education. Automated trading carries risk.” |
| Explicit exclusions | No return claim, no win rate, no balance screenshot, no urgency, no “passive income”, no “guaranteed”, no broker deposit call-to-action, no direct-message conversion. |
| Stop rule | Pause immediately upon rejection, policy warning, complaint, or a material content/landing-page change; re-review before any restart. |

## Quality-control checklist before approval

1. Confirm that every caption and visual avoids return, profit, guarantee, “passive income”, urgency, or personal-finance-status language.
2. Confirm that the asset uses only verified product names, prices, licence terms, and URLs.
3. Confirm that the risk notice is readable without relying on a hover state or a caption truncation.
4. Confirm that the landing-page destination is the public system briefing and risk notes, not payment or direct message.
5. Confirm the owner explicitly approves the exact post text, asset, destination URL, scheduled time, and campaign spend before publication.
