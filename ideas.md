# FizuxCoder Expert Advisors — Design Direction

## Three Initial Approaches

| Theme Name | Very Brief Intro | Probability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Signal Ledger** | A warm editorial financial brief that feels analytical, restrained, and transparent rather than promotional. | 0.07 |
| **Terminal Atelier** | A dark, material-rich software showcase that pairs technical interfaces with gallery-like product storytelling. | 0.04 |
| **Market Field Notes** | A tactile research-notebook approach that makes every claim feel annotated and methodical. | 0.09 |

## Chosen Approach — Signal Ledger

### Design Movement

**Swiss modernist editorial design** translated into a high-end financial-software briefing. The page should feel like a considered research publication rather than a generic trading landing page.

### Core Principles

1. **Evidence before hype.** Every product statement is anchored in a specific capability, while performance is framed as unverified historical information rather than a promise.
2. **Asymmetric editorial rhythm.** Large margins, off-axis panels, narrow annotation columns, and oversized typographic moments replace a conventional centered-card grid.
3. **Tactile precision.** Fine rules, paper grain, bracket marks, and restrained numerical styling create a sense of rigor and craft.
4. **Responsible conversion.** Primary calls to action explain the next step and disclose the Pepperstone referral relationship instead of applying artificial urgency.

### Color Philosophy

The base is warm **paper ivory** to establish clarity and trust, restrained **charcoal** provides editorial authority, and a deep **signal teal** carries interactive and navigational emphasis. **Saffron gold** is reserved for product pivots and decision markers, never used as a gratuitous profit cue. Contrast is deliberate and legible in every state.

### Layout Paradigm

The webpage is built as a **scrolling investment memo**: a large opening statement with a technical instrument to one side, followed by margin notes, split-feature sheets, a horizontal comparison ledger, and a final broker/setup protocol. Content should step between full-width editorial spreads and contained columns instead of uniform cards.

### Signature Elements

1. A vertical left-margin **edition rail** with section numbering and short annotations.
2. A thin **signal line** that tracks through sections and changes emphasis at product decisions.
3. **Bracketed data chips** and small source-style labels that distinguish implementation detail from general marketing copy.

### Interaction Philosophy

Interaction feels like reviewing a research dossier. Tabs reveal product differences, detail drawers expand only when requested, and an on-page allocation explorer supplies educational scenarios without presenting an investment forecast. Buttons make choices explicit and never imply guaranteed outcomes.

### Animation

Motion is quiet and purposeful: the edition rail traces in during initial load; content surfaces translate no more than 12px with opacity changes; signal points brighten during hover; tabs crossfade within 180–240ms. Animations use custom eased transitions, never auto-running performance counters, and respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

### Typography System

**DM Serif Display** handles product names and editorial statements, lending gravitas. **Manrope** is the body and UI face for clarity at small sizes. **IBM Plex Mono** marks settings, section indices, labels, and technical capability notes. Headlines use controlled mixed case; technical tags use uppercase mono with expanded tracking.

### Brand Essence

**FizuxCoder makes complex MT5 automation legible for traders who value structure, configurability, and risk awareness.**

Personality: **methodical, direct, composed**.

### Brand Voice

Headlines are precise and declarative; CTAs are explanatory; microcopy discloses material context clearly.

> “Automation, with the assumptions left visible.”

> “Review the architecture, then choose your setup path.”

### Wordmark & Logo

The brand mark is a three-stroke geometric signal: one path advances, one protects, and one frames a line of code. The mark appears beside a custom-spaced DM Serif wordmark rather than as default-font text.

### Signature Brand Color

**Signal Teal — #0E716E.** A deep, ownable teal chosen to communicate a validated decision signal rather than speculative excitement.
